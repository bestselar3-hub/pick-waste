# PickWaste product roadmap

## Phase 1 — MVP
- Customer booking form
- Driver assignment
- Pickup status transitions
- Route overview
- Admin monitoring dashboard
- Basic pricing and billing

## Phase 2 — Optimization
- Route clustering and optimization
- Recurring customers and subscriptions
- Better dispatch and map planning
- Service area definitions and dynamic pricing

## Phase 3 — Commercial scale
- B2B contracts
- Waste segregation tracking
- Fleet capacity analytics
- Multi-site management

## Phase 4 — Smart waste
- Smart bins and fill-level sensors
- Auto-triggered pickups
- Environmental impact reporting
- Sustainability insights

## Phase 5 — Regional expansion
- Multi-city operations
- Central dispatch control
- Regional analytics and financial reporting
